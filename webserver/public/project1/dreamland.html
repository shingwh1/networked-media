Dreamland

4th April 2021 - Sham Shui Po, Hong Kong

Dreamland was the album that made me fall into the Glass Animals rabbit hole. I listened to the album non-stop during COVID and during my IGCSE exams, because the psychedelic sound of the songs would calm me down in any situation.

Here are some of my favorites from the album:

  • Helium
  • Tokyo Drifting
  • Waterfalls Coming Out Your Mouth
  • Heat Waves
  • Domestic Bliss

Window to the World and myself

16th September 2026 - Brooklyn, New York

Having grown up with laptops since I was in elementary school, I’ve spent most of my conscious life with computers. On one hand I’m kind of curious how long I spend on my laptop, but on the other hand I’m scared of knowing just how long I spend staring at a screen everyday.

I think that if I took every laptop I’ve ever owned, it would tell a story of how I grew alongside this technology. My very first laptop I don’t even remember the brand of, but it was this ipad-sized laptop where I could play games. Then, the family computer where I learned about the online world and played flash games with friends. Even though our desktops or storage within these machines don’t occupy physical space, the files we leave behind are remnants of the life we lived beyond the virtual world, and I find that kind of cool.

Fire Drum

26th May 2014 - Kowloon Tong, Hong Kong

Today someone tricked me on Animal Jam. Someone came to my den, and asked me to trade them my fire drum. They told me that if I gave them my fire drum first, they would give me their super rare neon bow. I’ve been wanting to get the neon bow for the longest time, so obviously I said yes.

I gave traded them my fire drum for nothing, and they left my den immediately. I know fire drums aren’t the rarest, but I’m really upset that someone would trick me like that. At least now I know to not do that again.

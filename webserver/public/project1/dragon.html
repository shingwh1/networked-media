Year of the Dragon

24th February 2024 - New York, New York

The past week was my first Chinese New Year away from home. According to Feng Shui, the year of the dragon may bring fluctuating energy, and the horoscope for the monkey zodiac was that it might be a period of high highs and low lows. I miss home already, even though I’ve been back for less than a month. Second semester of freshman year is starting, and the work has already been a little crazy. It’s my first winter with snow, even though it really hasn’t snowed all that much yet. It get’s really cold in New York though, the wind is bad. The sun also sets at 4:00pm, so you’d literally go in for class in the morning, come out and the afternoon and the sky would be dark.

When we got back to SVA, me and Tifu would eat breakfast the first few days of class because we would both wake up at like 5am and have plenty of time before morning classes. Back to the new year thing, it’s a little strange that I’m not eating a big meal with all 30 of my relatives right now and getting free money, but I think it’s a good, new type of strange. I did go to dinner with all my roommates though, to Chinatown, and it did feel comforting even though we’re not technically family. We don’t come from the same place but it’s nice being able to share a piece of home with my friends who are also far from home.

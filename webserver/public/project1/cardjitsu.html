Card-jitsu

19th July 2015 - Kowloon Tong, Hong Kong

One of my favorite things to do is to play Card-jitsu on Club Penguin with my friends. It’s like rock paper scissors, but with fire, water and snow. You could also level up by playing more and earning the different colored belts like in martial arts. My favorite part was getting the elemental outfits, because I felt cool wearing them around so other people could see.

E for Ember

11th October 2017 - Prince Edward, Hong Kong

We were playing word chain on the bus today. It landed on “e” for me, and I said “ember” for my word. I learned it from the Pokemon game I was currently playing, Pokemon White 2, where one of Tepig’s starting fire moves was ember, and it was supereffective on grass types. My friend’s Dad told me that ember wasn’t a word, but I’m pretty sure it was and looked it up. It was a word.

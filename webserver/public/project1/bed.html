My First Double Bed

15th July 2025 - Brooklyn, New York

My entire life I’ve only ever had a single bed. Growing up in Hong Kong, space was always a luxury. I’ve always had a lofted single bed, where my working desk would go directly under the bed where the bottom bunk would normally go for the sake of making room for all the other furniture you’re supposed to fit in a room. At our current apartment back home, my lofted bed is so high up that I can easily touch the ceiling from my bed. Between the wardrobes and the bed/table, there isn’t enough space to roll out a yoga mat in the same direction to the bed.

When I moved in to my current place, the previous tenant had left a full bed behind which I inherited. It was weird at first, and I still only sleep on one side of the bed, but I’ve grown to love it even though it takes up most of the floor space in my already small room. I just find it funny that New York, of all places was the first place where I’d ever have a big bed to myself.
